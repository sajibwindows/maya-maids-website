About Maya Maids

House help you don't have to worry about.

Maya Maids started with a simple frustration many Dhaka households know well — finding a maid you can trust, who shows up on time, and stays. We built a network of background-verified, trained home help so you can hand over the keys with confidence, not anxiety.

Every maid on our platform goes through identity verification, reference checks and a hands-on training module before ever stepping into your home. If something isn't working, we replace — no long arguments, no guesswork.
